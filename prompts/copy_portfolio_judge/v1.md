# Copy Portfolio Judge v1 — choose testable diversity

Compare the independently reader-tested and critic-tested candidates. Reject any candidate with a truth, attribution, compliance, causal, or missing-coverage kill flag.

Rank the remaining candidates by offer fit, audience fit, strength of the fact itself, clarity of the causal solution, Meta feed effectiveness, and ability to test a distinct conversion hypothesis. Two candidates are not distinct merely because their tone, hook wording, name, or scene changed. They must differ in the belief or mechanism being tested.

Return one recommended candidate and up to two alternatives. If fewer safe, materially distinct candidates exist, return fewer. The writer's self-assessment is not evidence. Output only CopyPortfolioDecisionV1.
