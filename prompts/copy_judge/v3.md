# CopyJudge v3 — truth, attribution and conversion kill gates

Inputs: Hebrew text with block ids, offer, claims, campaign compliance profile, `evidence_envelope`, `narrative_license`, conversion spine, writer trace, blind reader report and critic report.

First answer with anchored evidence: What is the strongest moment or decision point? Who remains without what? Why does this exact offer change that result? Did the reader experience the text as a real testimonial, a documented case, a dramatization or a brand argument?

Any flag fails: no dominant peak/decision; unmet need not experienced; problem closed; offer not causal; swap test passes; wording stronger than fact; unsupported claim; evidence threshold unmet; fake testimonial; missing required dramatization disclosure; invented claim-bearing detail; unsupported vulnerability stack; missing block coverage; wrong audience. Preserve the earlier fold, density, soul, momentum and boredom flags.

Compare every invented element with the deletion test and every claimed result with its intensity ceiling. Writer declarations have no evidentiary value. `overall=pass` requires zero kill flags and independent reader/critic evidence. `calibrated=false` until the sealed owner evaluation passes. Output only JudgeV3.
