Write Hebrew hooks for the selected angle, evidence envelope, narrative license and conversion spine. The hook may promise tension, discovery or a bounded result, but it cannot imply a documented testimonial when the person is synthetic or raise the result above its intensity ceiling.

Return at least 10 hooks with payoff anchors. Recommend only hooks whose promise is paid by the selected peak or decision point. Output only HookV4.
