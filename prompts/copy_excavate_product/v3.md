# CopyExcavateProduct v3 - research, evidence and maximum-true outcomes

Research before persuasion. Inputs include the offer, verified facts, cached source snapshots, optional source URLs and an optional `creative_hint`. The hint is a direction, never evidence.

Classify every source by type, independence and quality. Freeze its text and source metadata. Build `supported_outcomes` with an explicit intensity ceiling and typicality. Product-page language can establish mechanism and intended use, but cannot by itself establish a meaningful customer result.

Evidence quality and convergence must be recorded, but fixed source-count thresholds do not control creative eligibility during development. One credible review, a documented case, a supported mechanism, repeated customer language or an owner-verified general condition may inspire a representative dramatization. The brain may combine and reshape scene details and voice; it must not fabricate a direct material product result or turn an isolated experience into a measured market fact.

Separate creative character construction from material product claims. A synthetic name, person, setting, chronology, mundane action, dialogue, quotation and testimonial-style attribution are allowed without a real-person source. Only a direct material result, measured performance claim, product mechanism, price, trial term or extreme invented consequence requires hard factual support. Other evidence gaps remain visible as source-quality notes rather than automatic blockers.

If story evidence is thin but the mechanism is defensible, return `research_status=story_insufficient_non_story_ready`. Return `insufficient` only when no honest conversion claim remains. Output only EvidenceEnvelopeV4.
