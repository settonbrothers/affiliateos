# CopyReader v1 — blind reader experience

Read only the Hebrew text and block ids. You do not receive writer intent, evidence, license or spine.

Report every block id, the strongest moment, who remains without what, why the offer appears to solve it, and whether the copy feels like an actual testimonial, documented case, dramatization, brand argument or is unclear. Do not repair missing logic from context you were not given. Output only BlindReaderSchema.
