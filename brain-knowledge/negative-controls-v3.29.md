# Negative controls v3.29

## Round5 - components without reader experience

Round5 contained a story, meal delivery, scarcity, a number and a CTA. It still failed.

The protagonist had already received the meal and visit. The emotional peak concerned loneliness and television. The offer guaranteed meal delivery. The later number inherited the delivery mechanism but not the lived problem or emotional capital.

Required failure diagnosis:

- no dominant powerful peak;
- no live material need experienced in the scene;
- the offer does not directly solve the problem the reader felt;
- component presence was mistaken for causality.

## LeadEcho Opus 5 - better prose with missing brain and false approval

The proof writer produced a functional product argument. It was not the deep first-person anchor story the owner expected. Five learning references remained unresolved in the actual writer prompt, and no approved Taste example was loaded.

The copy also expanded sources:

- an observation became "אלה לא אנשים שהתעקשו והתקשרו שוב" without evidence;
- approval of message text became "בניסוח שלך, לא של רובוט";
- "מה שכולם עושים" and "שאף אחד לא מסתכל עליו" created generic unsupported authority;
- several defensive negatives cooled the movement before the CTA.

All automated agents approved it. That approval is itself a negative control. Multiple agents sharing the same vocabulary are not independent when all test component presence instead of reader causality and felt quality.

## Failure rule

The following candidate must not become ready:

- mechanically clean but emotionally flat;
- a character wrapper around a product presentation;
- a strong hook with a body that does not repay it;
- dramatic wording around a weak event;
- a story whose emotional problem differs from the product result;
- a proof claim stronger than its source relationship;
- a candidate that regresses below the applicable Michael v5 capabilities;
- a candidate approved only because writer, reader, critic and judge repeat the same doctrine labels.
