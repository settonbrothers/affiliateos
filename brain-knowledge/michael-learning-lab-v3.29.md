## V2 to V3 - make the human consequence direct

Earlier wording softened the consequence: "זה חצי מהסיבה שהוא קם בבוקר". The correction made the absence and change direct: "מאז שרינה נפטרה לא היה לו בשביל מה לקום בבוקר - עד שהבחורה הזאת התחילה להגיע".

Lesson: do not weaken the strongest supported human cost with protective phrasing. The fact itself must carry the force.

Writer question: what changed in this person's actual day, and have I said the strongest version the evidence allows?

When not: do not intensify beyond the evidence or turn an incidental benefit into a guaranteed product result.

## V3 to V4 - create curiosity before explanation

The earlier version opened with exposition. The stronger version entered through distance and anomaly: "שני מטר" and the relationship between the shiva chair and the neighbor's door.

Lesson: the reader enters a scene with a live question before receiving context. Each answer opens the next question.

Writer question: why must the reader know the next line right now?

When not: a short, high-awareness argument can use a direct decision point instead of a long mystery.

## V4 to V5 - make the need visible in action

The earlier dialogue stopped after "כל יום. כבר שנים." The added exchange about who cooked, fifty years of Rina, and "עכשיו יש את השקית" made the absence material and emotionally specific.

Lesson: a delivery mechanism is not a need. Show the before, the loss and what the present action now carries.

Writer question: where did the reader experience the unmet need rather than merely hear that it exists?

When not: do not add biography or vulnerability only to force emotion.

## Why V5 passed

- The opening creates a personal fear and a visual question.
- The narrator learns rather than lectures.
- Repeated daily beats accumulate meaning.
- The emotional realization turns inward: the narrator fears who will knock on his own door.
- The organization appears only after the story earns the need for an explanation.
- The offer funds the exact mechanism carrying the emotional meaning.
- The people on the waiting list preserve the open problem at the ask.
- The CTA gives one person agency at two honest price levels.

## Why the reference is not a template

Do not reuse bereavement, a door, daily knocking, an elderly neighbor, a flight home, a late-night message, a waiting list or the same paragraph silhouette unless the new offer independently creates those facts and needs them.

Transfer the capabilities: curiosity, scene, accumulation, dominant realization, causal product entry, open need and a human action.
